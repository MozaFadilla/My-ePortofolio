Course Self-Reflection

Reflection of Learning

Synthesizing challenges, personal strengths, growth areas, and real-world pharmacy application from English 3 KJ08.

Challenge 1: Technical Vocabulary vs. Conciseness

As a pharmacy student, I frequently defaulted to overly complex scientific jargon when communicating ideas. In English 3 KJ08, structuring concise short proposals and formal emails was initially difficult because I struggled to condense technical pharmacological concepts for non-expert audiences.

How I Overcame It:

I adopted the BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) approach in emails and utilized plain-language summaries prior to scientific breakdowns. Practicing peer reviews in class helped refine my brevity.

Challenge 2: Presentation Anxiety & Body Language

Delivering live oral presentations caused fidgeting and monotonous tone. Managing non-verbal cues—such as eye contact, open posture, and pacing—while explaining complex research slides was a significant hurdle.

How I Overcame It:

I utilized video mock presentations to record my non-verbal body language. Applying purposeful pauses and pre-planned slide triggers helped reduce anxiety and built my resilience under pressure.

Identified Strengths

  • Structured Academic Writing: Strong capability in crafting well-organized short proposals, logically breaking down problems, objectives, and solutions.
  • Adaptive Dialogue: Ability to adjust tone fluently depending on context—shifting between formal professional emails and collaborative oral communication.
  • Visual Presentation Design: High aesthetic sense in slide design, keeping visual clutter low to emphasize core pharmacological metrics.

Focus Areas for Continuous Improvement

  • Impromptu Speaking Agility: Expanding vocal confidence and clarity when answering unexpected questions during Q&A sessions.
  • Vocal Variety & Cadence: Incorporating dynamic pitch and pacing variations to prevent vocal monotony during technical lectures.
  • Active Non-verbal Consistency: Maintaining relaxed shoulder alignment and deliberate hand gestures throughout long presentations.
